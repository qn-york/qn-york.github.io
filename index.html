Queer Nerds York

Looking for queer friends in York?

We’re a small, informal social group for LGBTQ+ adults in York, particularly people in their late 20s and 30s with broadly nerdy interests. We’ve built up a small group of regulars with a mix of genders, identities and interests, and new faces are always welcome. We meet roughly once a month for coffee and a chat.

Interested in coming along?

Please message Rhys or Ross first. We'll let you know the details of the next meetup, and you're welcome to ask questions!

Coming along for the first time?

We’re usually a small group of around 6 to 10 people, and newcomers are always welcome.

When you arrive, look for the little stuffed rabbit on our table, that's how you'll know you've found us!

Who’s organising this?

Hi, I'm Rhys! I'm 35 and have lived in York for 15 years. Ross and I started these meetups together in January 2026 because we wanted to make more queer friends locally and felt there weren't many casual queer social groups outside the universities. My interests are science fiction, architecture, and I edit Wikipedia!
